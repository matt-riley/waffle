# Shared brand brief (all design concepts)

## Project

**Waffle** is a personal project: a personal AI agent (Go, one binary, chat, tools, sandbox, gateway). It is **named after Matt’s beautiful, lively young kitten**.

## Voice (non-negotiable)

**Do:** human, upbeat, affectionate, a little mischievous; “my agent”, “a project”, “named after my cat”.

**Do not:** marketing clichés (elevate, unleash, seamless, next-gen, revolutionize); hard-sell CTAs; self-host slogan-speak; hero lines like “on your hardware”, “your metal”, “runs on your machine”; AI SaaS swagger; fake conversion metrics.

Technical facts (sandbox, binary, chat) may appear as small secondary text only.
