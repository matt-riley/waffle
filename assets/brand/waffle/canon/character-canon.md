# Waffle Character Canon

This document defines the identity constraints shared by Hero Waffle and Motion Waffle. The layered vector master is the production source of truth; generated studies and rendered exports are derivatives.

## Character identity

Waffle is an **eternal kitten** and **cat first**. Her default anatomy, locomotion, balance, and resting poses must read as feline. A brief two-legged gesture, wave, pointed paw, held prop, panel peek, or interface interaction is allowed when it returns naturally to a feline stance. She is never a permanently upright, human-shaped mascot.

Her personality is curious, affectionate, mischievous, and unmistakably a little princess. Expressions should feel alert and warm rather than vacant, aggressive, or exaggerated into generic cartoon shorthand.

## Non-negotiable identity anchors

- Warm ginger coat with darker classic-tabby swirls.
- Strong forehead M and cheek stripes.
- Pale muzzle, chin, eye surrounds, and belly.
- Grey-green eyes and a pink nose.
- Large ears and long whiskers.
- Banded legs and a distinctly ringed tail.
- Long, slightly lanky kitten proportions.

Every approved view and pose must preserve the forehead M, grey-green eye colour, pale muzzle, leg bands, broken flank swirls, ringed tail, and kitten proportions. Shading and fur detail may change by tier; these anchors may not.

## Vector construction ratios

These are **vector construction ratios**, not biological measurements. They keep the stylized character consistent across views and poses.

| Feature | Ratio |
| --- | --- |
| Body length | `3.1 head widths` |
| Shoulder height | `1.45 head heights` |
| Tail length | `0.9 body lengths` |
| Ear height | `0.48 head heights` |
| Eye separation | `0.42 eye widths` |

Measure body length from chest to rump before the tail. Measure shoulder height from the standing baseline. Tail length follows the tail centreline. Eye separation is the clear gap between the inner eye edges.

## Marking map

- Centre the forehead M between the ears and connect it visually to narrow crown stripes without turning it into a solid cap.
- Keep cheek stripes distinct from the pale muzzle and eye surrounds.
- Use broken, asymmetric classic-tabby swirls on the flanks rather than evenly spaced tiger bars.
- Carry bands around the legs while keeping the paws readable.
- Make tail rings clear at documentation size, with a darker terminal band.
- Preserve the pale chin and belly as one coherent identity family without flattening the torso into a single cream patch.

## Do and do not

Do preserve feline weight, balance, joint direction, and a long kitten silhouette. Do simplify fur into readable planes and keep expression changes concentrated in ears, eyelids, eyes, muzzle, and tail.

Do not shorten Waffle into a chibi body, broaden her into an adult-cat build, replace the classic-tabby pattern with generic stripes, change the eye colour, remove the pale muzzle, or give her human hands, clothing, or a permanent biped stance.
