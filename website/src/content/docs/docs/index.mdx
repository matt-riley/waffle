## Two ways in

**Meet Waffle** is written for anyone. It assumes you know how to open a
terminal and nothing else. Start at [What Waffle is](/docs/meet/what-waffle-is/)
and read straight through.

**Under the hood** is for when you want exact paths, exact keys, and no
hand-holding. Every plain-language page ends with a link into its technical
counterpart, so you can drop down at the moment you need to and not before.

## A word on where this is

Waffle is a developer preview. Tagged releases exist, but config keys, flags,
and stored data can still change between them. Sharp edges are expected, and
these pages will say so where they matter rather than pretending otherwise.
