# Design Context — Waffle website

## Brand personality

Warm, affectionate, curious, a little mischievous. Personal project energy — not a product launch. Named after Matt’s lively young ginger kitten (little-princess, cat-first).

## Page job

Introduce **Waffle** clearly: a personal AI agent project. Soft path to source. Charm over conversion.

## Audience

Matt and people who already care. Clarity and warmth, not persuasion funnels.

## Locked direction

**Concept A — Sunlit Kitten**

- Cream paper, morning window light
- Character-first (approved brand PNGs)
- Asymmetric split hero
- Gallery air (low visual density)

## Typography

| Role | Face | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display | **Nunito** | Soft rounded grotesk — human, friendly, not Inter/SaaS |
| Body | **Source Serif 4** | Calm literary body; personal narrative, not dashboard |

Not reflex fonts. Do not swap to Inter/DM Sans/Fraunces without a new decision.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| paper | `#FBF7F0` | Page ground |
| paper-warm | `#F5EDE0` | Soft bands |
| ink | `#1A1612` | Primary text |
| ink-muted | `#5C4A3A` | Secondary |
| label | `#8D481F` | Section labels |
| ginger | `#E99A42` | **Only** accent (CTA, rules) |
| ginger-light | `#F5C579` | Soft wash / hover |

No AI purple/blue. No pure black `#000`. No gradient text.

## Motion dials

| Dial | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Variance | 5–6 |
| Motion intensity | 3–4 |
| Density | 2–3 |

- One-shot hero entrance + soft scroll reveals
- **No** perpetual toy motion (no infinite cat bob)
- **No** scroll hijack, sticky stacks, marquees
- Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`

## Absolute bans (copy)

- “Not a product launch” (and variants)
- Hero subline that only says “named after a kitten” (undercuts what it is)
- Hardware slogans (“on your hardware”, “your metal”, “runs on your machine”)
- Marketing clichés (elevate, unleash, seamless, next-gen…)

## Assets

Approved art only from brand tree / `src/assets/waffle/`. Do not invent a new cat.

## Stack

Astro + Tailwind v4 + GSAP (ScrollTrigger). Stay on this stack.

## Impeccable bans checklist

- [ ] No side-stripe borders on cards
- [ ] No gradient text
- [ ] Fonts justified above
- [ ] Hero uses `min-h-[100dvh]` not `h-screen`
